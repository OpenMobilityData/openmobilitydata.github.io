BikeStat

Traffic counts from a curated set of sensors in Côte-des-Neiges–Notre-Dame-de-Grâce (City counters, Telraam, the borough's loop counter as released under the Act) on one chart, hour to month, with year-on-year comparison; English and French.

bikestat.org Folder and sources

Web application

AirQualityMTL

The City's air-quality network (RSQA) hourly measurements 1986–2024, as an interpolated map by pollutant and period and as time series by station.
