Public records and open data on mobility in Montréal

Documents and datasets kept with their provenance, and the code that reads them.

Most of the material was assembled around one question: the protected bike lane on rue de Terrebonne, in Côte-des-Neiges–Notre-Dame-de-Grâce, and the borough's plan of October 2026 to convert one direction to parking each winter. Much of it is city-wide and is meant to outlast that question: police-reported collisions for the whole island, vehicle registrations, the City's access-to-information releases, the City's bicycle counters.

Every entry links to a folder whose README says what the document is, who issued it, where and when it was obtained, and how each table and figure can be reproduced.
